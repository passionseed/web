# PS[01]: first 10 (simple version)

Two print files, one DTF print shop, about a week. The web version is at `/merch/ps01`.

## The prints

| File | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| [print/front-chest.png](print/front-chest.png) | 10.6 × 2.4 cm | Left chest: top edge ~8 cm below the shoulder seam, ~4 cm from the centre line |
| [print/back-30x36cm.png](print/back-30x36cm.png) | 30 × 36 cm | Centred, top edge ~7 cm below the back collar seam |

Both are transparent PNGs at 300 dpi, already at print size. `print/render.js` regenerates them; copies for the website are in `public/merch/ps01/`.

- **Front:** the PassionSeed logo + "PassionSeed" in Kodchasan, the same lockup as the site header.
- **Back:** "PassionSeed", a few pixel stars, the full logo as the sun, sitting on the pixel Dusk horizon.

## Steps

1. **Tees (day 1).** Get sizes from the 10 people by trying a tee on. Buy 11 dark cotton tees (10 + 1 test), black or dark purple. Bobae has every size.
2. **Print shop (day 1–2).** Send both files to a DTF shop on LINE: 11 sets, and ask them to press the prints onto your tees.
   > สวัสดีครับ ขอทำ DTF 11 ชุด ชุดละ 2 ลาย: ลายหน้าอก 10.6 × 2.4 ซม. และลายหลัง 30 × 36 ซม. (ไฟล์ PNG พื้นใส 300 dpi แนบมาครับ) รบกวนรีดลงเสื้อให้ด้วย 11 ตัว ขอราคาและวันรับของครับ
3. **Test (day 3–5).** Wash the test shirt 3 times inside out. If the print holds, hand out the other 10.

If the shop gives you transfers to press yourself: cotton setting, no steam, a hard table, baking paper on top, press hard 15–20 s per spot without sliding, let it cool, peel slowly, then 5 more seconds everywhere.

With the 4ply yarn: crochet the 16px pixel logo, one square per single crochet (chart on the web page). About 7–8 cm on a 2.5 mm hook.

The pocket, velcro patch panel, NFC tag, locker loop and glow stars from the tech pack stay as ideas for the bigger run. They're not needed for the first 10.
