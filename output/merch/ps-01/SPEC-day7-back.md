# PS-01 Day 7: back art pick (round 2 renders)

Renders: `gen/day7/codex/`, `gen/day7/agy/`. Brief: `gen/BRIEF-day7-renders.md`. Judged by 4 lenses (art, logo/text, garment/production, wearer), a synthesis and a skeptic.

## Pick
Back B, the original comic scene (the seed at a desk late on Day 7), using the codex generator's version (codex-B-back.png and codex-B-print-detail.png) as the base direction. It is a concept to redraw, not finished art.

**Runner-up:** Back A from codex (codex-A-back.png and codex-A-print-detail.png). It is not the back to print. It is the reference for logo fidelity and tone mapping: crescent-tongue flame, a grainy base with solid bright tongues, a tongue that breaks the panel with 3 ticks, a faint disc arc, and the only correct tally on a back (4 + slash + 2). If B's illustrator commission or its discharge strike-off fails, fall back to this layout, with its seed, grin and fuse defects fixed and the wordmark scaled down. Both agy backs are out. agy-A adds a second (orange) ink and a grin inside the flame. agy-B is a flood-filled cream rectangle at 65 to 75% coverage, drawn in clip-art style with halftone dots.

## Why
Weighted scores for each back (art 35 / logo 25 / wearer 25 / garment 15):
- codex-B-back: 7.18 (7.5 / 6 / 8 / 7)
- codex-A-back: 6.65 (6 / 8 / 6 / 7)
- agy-B-back: 5.10
- agy-A-back: 4.43

The detail images rank the same way: codex-B-detail 6.93, codex-A-detail 6.48.

Codex-B wins on the two heaviest lenses that touch the founder's complaint. It is the only back that reads as illustration-led art (woodcut or photocopy grit, a dry-brush panel edge, burgundy left as night, a scene a stranger understands as "all-nighter") and not as "logo but bigger". The founder has already rejected the centred-mascot-over-wordmark stack twice ("boring", "fast fashion"), and both A backs are that stack. Codex-A scores higher only on logo fidelity. That lead is not structural: B's flame and head can be redrawn from logo.png geometry. A's layout problem cannot be fixed without turning it into B.

Production does not block B. Codex draws it cream on burgundy, with the night as bare fabric, so it is still one discharge screen. Coverage is about 45 to 50% as rendered and can be brought to 40% or less by cutting props.

I checked the codex-B tally myself at zoom. It is 5 verticals plus a slash plus 2, so it reads as 8 marks; codex-A-back is the only correct one. B's real weaknesses are fixable in the redraw:
- a campfire flame, not the logo flame
- a hoodie torso (the "lofi-girl" trope)
- the face lost among too many props and occluded by the laptop
- round halftone shading
- a border line running through the breaking tongue in the detail image
- a crescent moon that echoes the flame crescents
- an oversized print, about 39cm wide against 26cm

The brief below removes each one.

## Defects to fix in the redraw

Redraw it from logo.png: one big flame mass leaning up and to the right, an orange crescent tongue on the upper left, a bright crescent in the centre, a large hooked tongue at the top right with a bright crescent inside it, a small crescent at the top, and the orange tongue dipping into the top of the seed.

There must be no U-shaped curve or keylined crescent below or across the eyes. Break the flame under the seed with grain falloff, not a solid crescent.

- Brows: the left brow merges into the flame (codex-A-detail, agy-A), and the brows are scowling or angry (codex-B, agy-A). Use thin arched brows as in the logo, knocked out to fabric with a 1-cell keyline where they cross the flame. Tired or focused acting can come from the lids, not an angry wedge.

## Final back-art brief (v1, see skeptic fixes below)
FORMAT
- One colour, cream #EDE3CC (colour-discharge with pigment), on burgundy #5E1B2C linen-cotton slub. The art is 1-bit: cream or nothing. No second ink, no halftone dots, no flood fills.
- Art box: 26cm wide x 30cm tall on a size M, centred on centre back. Top of the box 6cm below the back yoke seam.
- Coverage: 40% or less of the art box (aim for 32 to 38%).
- Delivery: 600ppi 1-bit film, built from a 20 px/inch threshold grid (1.27mm cells) upsampled nearest-neighbour. Minimum line 0.7mm; minimum isolated grain cluster is 1 cell. Print on 110 to 125 mesh.

LAYOUT (cm from the top-left of the art box)
- Flame breakout zone: y 0 to 3, x 15 to 26. The top-right tongue rises about 2.5cm above the panel's top edge. Three motion ticks sit outside the break at the upper right, each 1 to 1.4cm long and 2mm wide, fanned at 20, 45 and 70 degrees.
- Panel: x 1 to 25 (24cm wide), y 2.5 to 22.5 (20cm tall). Dry-brush border, 2.5 to 3.5mm wide, wobbling plus or minus 1mm, with 2 or 3 small dry-brush breaks along its length. The border stops cleanly on both sides of the breaking tongue, leaving a gap of about 4cm in the top edge at x 17 to 21. Nothing crosses the tongue.
- Caption: an outlined box 4.5 x 1.6cm at x 2 to 6.5, y 3.5 to 5.1, with a 1.5mm cream outline. 'DAY 7' in solid upright pixel serif, 0.9cm cap height. Not reversed out.
- Wordmark: 'PassionSeed' in an upright stepped pixel serif (a DM Serif or Caslon-class OFL face rasterised at an 18 px cap height, no anti-aliasing). About 20cm wide, 2.4cm cap height (cell about 1.3mm), centred, baseline at y 27. Measure the real advance width and set the width only.
- Tally: bottom right at x 20.5 to 25, y 27.8 to 29.8. Hand-scratched 1.5 to 2mm strokes, 2cm tall: exactly 4 verticals, 1 diagonal slash across them, a gap, then 2 verticals. No numeral.

SCENE (inside the panel, burgundy = night, cream = light only)
- The seed sits at a desk edge that runs across the lower quarter of the panel (y about 17.5).
- Seed: the logo's tilted black egg, about 7cm tall, placed right of centre (centre at about x 14.5, y 13), so the face is the focal point and sits on the panel's upper-right third line.
  - The seed is open fabric. Its silhouette reads by contrast: put a solid 1.5 to 2mm cream rim-light on the side facing the laptop, and a lamp-lit wall behind the head as grain at about 30 to 35% (this replaces agy's flood fill).
  - Eyes: big solid-cream googly whites overlapping the seed's left and right edges, as in logo.png. Pupils are fabric, dead centre.
  - Brows: thin arched cream brows. Where a brow crosses the flame it is knocked out to fabric with a 1-cell keyline.
  - No mouth, no fuse or stem, no torso, no hoodie. The seed is the body: two single-stroke noodle arms (1.2mm line) with mitten hands on the keyboard. Pose after agy-B's gesture: hunched forward, eyes on the screen.
- Flame: drawn from logo.png geometry, rising from the top of the seed and leaning up and to the right. The big hooked top-right tongue flares through the border gap. Tone map, one value per part:

  | Part | Ink |
  |---|---|
  | Flame base | 55 to 62%, photocopy grain, falling to about 40% at the lower left |
  | Bright crescents (centre and inside the top-right hook) | 100% solid |
  | Orange tongues (upper left, and the one dipping into the seed's crown) | about 30% sparse grain, as filled tapered crescents, never a line |
  | Under the seed | grain fades out; no solid cream cup and no U-curve under the eyes |
  | Edges | 1-cell no-ink keyline between all 100% shapes (tongues, eye whites) and the grain |

- Laptop: three-quarter view at the lower left of the seed (x 6 to 12, y 13.5 to 18), lid angled so its back faces the viewer's left. The screen glow is a hard-edged wedge of grain at about 45% that lights the face from below and left. Nothing occludes the eyes. An optional flame sticker on the lid must be a solid silhouette of at least 1.5cm; otherwise drop it. No other logo.
- Lamp: anglepoise at the far left (x 2 to 6). Its light cone is a hard-edged trapezoid down to the desk, filled with grain at 25 to 35%; the shade underside is solid.
- Mug: one mug at the right of the seed on the desk (about 2cm tall), with 2 single-line steam wisps.
- Sticky notes: at most 2 on the wall at the upper left, under the caption.
- Window: at the right edge (x 19 to 24, y 6 to 14), frame in 1mm lines. Inside, a Bangkok skyline: low-rise shophouse rooflines, one sagging power line, a scatter of lit windows as solid 1 to 2-cell squares. A round moon or no moon; never a crescent.
- Drop: books, loose papers, the second cup, extra notes, the hoodie, every halftone dot, the sky gradient.

LINEWORK
- Confident brush or nib ink, variable 0.8 to 2.5mm, with a slightly chewed photocopy edge. Woodcut-like cut lines on the desk edge and wall corner are allowed.
- Comic motion only at the flame breakout (the 3 ticks) and 2 or 3 short energy dashes beside the flame. No sparkles, stars, hearts, swirls or comets (method from Boy In A Dream only, no borrowed drawings).

GRAIN METHOD (code or illustrator scan)
- Paint or vector the scene as greyscale values per the tone map.
- Add stochastic noise (Perlin plus white, plus or minus 18%) at 1-cell scale, threshold at 50%, and output 1-bit at 20 px/inch (1.27mm cells). Hard stair-stepped edges on the flame silhouette, as in the star-bitmap reference.
- Proof at 1:1 on paper. Check at 2m that both eyes, both brows, the tongues and the seed silhouette read, and ask 3 people who do not know the brand what they see.

WORN AND MARKETING
- Shoot the lookbook with Back B in a plain lived-in Bangkok soi at dusk (wires, street cart, sodium lamp).
- No tuk-tuk, no temple spire, no souvenir signifiers.

## Skeptic (holds)
The pick holds, but only just, and the redraw brief needs repair before it goes to an illustrator. Back B is the right direction for the founder's "art taste" complaint. Of the four backs, only agy-B and codex-B read as illustration rather than a bigger logo, and both A backs repeat the logo-over-wordmark stack of concept-1. Three things weaken the case for B. First, codex-B's appeal comes from the parts the brief removes: the campfire flame worn as hair, the hunched hoodie and a dense lo-fi prop set. After a logo-faithful redraw it becomes a different drawing that nobody has seen yet, so its 7.18 score does not carry over. Second, the brief contains factual and technical errors that would wreck the print: the pupil rule, the seed shape, the noise and threshold maths, and line weights below the grid cell. Third, it never says how a flame drawn from logo.png can sit on a 7cm seed inside a scene without filling the panel. A is not better as a direction. But the spec told the reviewers to make both backs and let the founder choose. The synthesis instead shows the founder only B, plus a worn photo that actually shows Back A with a temple spire and a tuk-tuk. That needs fixing.

### Risks
- The pupil rule is wrong. I cropped logo.png: neither pupil is centred. Both sit about a quarter of the eye width to the right (left pupil centre about x136 against an eye centre of about x103 in the crop; right pupil about x421 against x382), so the mascot looks right. The brief's 'pupils dead centre, as in logo.png' contradicts the logo, and its defect list marks the faithful renders as wrong. It also contradicts the brief's own acting note, 'eyes on the screen', because centred pupils make the seed stare at the viewer instead of the laptop.
- The seed shape description is wrong. In logo.png the seed is broad and round at the top and tapers toward the lower left. It is not an egg with a 'narrow top'. An illustrator who follows the brief will draw the egg upside down.
- The tone map prints nothing in the light tones. A 50% threshold with ±18% noise is a steep step, not a dither. Every grey below 32% produces no ink at all, and every grey above 68% prints solid. So the 25 to 35% lamp cone, the 30 to 35% lamp-lit wall behind the head and the ~30% 'sparse grain' orange tongues all disappear. The 55 to 62% flame base prints at roughly 64 to 83% coverage and reads as near-solid from 2m, the blob problem again, and the 45% screen glow prints at about 36%. The spec's skeptic review set the orange tongues at 30% and the synthesis repeated the mistake.
- Figure-ground for the face is broken. Making burgundy the night turns the seed into bare fabric surrounded by more bare fabric. agy-B, the one render where the face reads from 3m, works because a black seed sits against a cream wall. The brief keeps agy-B's 'big black seed silhouette' but its own method removes it. The two things meant to replace it fail: the lamp-lit wall grain prints nothing (see the risk above), and a 1.5 to 2mm rim-light is too thin to carry the silhouette from across a room.
- A flame drawn from the logo does not fit the scene. In logo.png the flame is about 2.5 to 2.7 times the seed's height and width, and it wraps under the seed. With a 7cm seed, a faithful flame is about 17 to 19cm across on a 24 x 20cm panel. It would run over the window (x 19 to 24, y 6 to 14), the border gap and the lamp. The brief asks for logo geometry and a 7cm seed and a full scene, and also cuts the flame's under-wrap ('grain fades out'). In practice the illustrator must either shrink the flame into generic hair, which loses the logo, or let it swallow the props, at which point B turns back into A with a desk.
- Shrinking the print to its real size hurts B much more than A. codex-B is rendered about 36 to 39cm wide. At 26cm (about 0.67x) the face is a small share of the panel, each eye is about 1.3 to 1.5cm, a pupil is about 3 to 4 cells of 1.27mm, and the lit windows are 1 or 2 cells. Seen from 2 to 3m, the scene turns into texture with a wordmark under it. codex-A's single large mascot survives the same reduction. Nobody has looked at a 1:1 proof of B at 26cm yet.
- The line weights cannot exist on the grid. The brief requires the film to be 'built from a 20 px/inch threshold grid (1.27mm cells) upsampled nearest-neighbour', yet it specifies a 0.7mm minimum line, 0.8mm brush lines, a 1mm window frame and 1.2mm noodle arms. On a 1.27mm grid nothing thinner than 1.27mm exists, so every one of these lines snaps to 1.27mm or vanishes.
- The layout collides with itself. The left strip (x 1 to 6.5) has to hold the caption (y 3.5 to 5.1), two sticky notes under it, and an anglepoise lamp rising from the desk at y 17.5 with its arm and shade up in that same zone. The laptop (x 6 to 12, top at y 13.5) runs into the seed's left eye: the seed is centred at x 14.5 and the eyes stick out past its edges to about x 10.5, at about y 11 to 14. The tally (x 20.5 to 25, y 27.8) sits 0.8cm under the wordmark's last letters (wordmark about x 3 to 23, baseline y 27), so the two will read as one cluttered line.
- The scene has three light sources: the flame, the lamp and the screen. That is visual noise, and it misses the scene's strongest idea, which is that the seed's head already is the light. Desk, anglepoise lamp, mug, night window and city skyline are also the Lofi Girl / study-with-me composition. Removing the hoodie does not remove that trope, and the trope is exactly the 'not a cool garment' risk the founder named.
- The brand message is risky. The shirt celebrates an all-nighter on Day 7 and will be worn by minors, and the people buying are parents. A lone character grinding at a desk at night can read as burnout culture rather than 'a real project with your team'.
- The synthesis overrides the spec's process. The spec's skeptic review says to prototype both backs and let the founder pick. images_to_show_founder includes no A image at all. It does include codex-worn, which shows Back A, oversized, with a tuk-tuk and a temple spire (both banned motifs). The founder would be judging B from a worn photo of A.
- One diagnosis is probably wrong. agy-B's 'dark maroon linework' looks like fabric showing through a flood fill (one ink, knocked out), not a second ink. The real fault in agy-B is the 65 to 75% flood coverage. agy-A's orange tongues are a genuine second ink.
- The claim that the founder has 'already rejected the centred-mascot-over-wordmark stack twice' is not in any merch document I searched. The only recorded feedback is 'no art taste' and 'fast fashion'. concept-1 (the logo over the wordmark) suggests the claim is plausible, but it is an inference and should not drive the decision.

### Fixes (apply over the brief above)
- Correct the pupil rule. Pupils sit offset toward the gaze target, as in logo.png, where both pupils sit about 25% of the eye width to the right. In Back B, aim both pupils down and left at the laptop. Never put them dead centre. Remove 'pupils off-centre' from the defect list.
- Correct the seed description: a rounded blob, broad at the top, tapering toward the lower left, with the eye whites overlapping its left and right edges. Tell the illustrator to trace the silhouette directly from logo.png.
- Replace the grey percentages with target ink coverage, and use a dither that delivers it: blue-noise or error-diffusion stochastic screening at the 1.27mm cell, or noise of about ±50% before the 50% threshold. Targets: flame base 45 to 55% coverage, falling to 25% at the lower left. Orange tongues 15 to 20%. Light cone and screen glow 12 to 20%. Wall halo 20 to 25%. Before burning any film, verify on the actual output bitmap by counting cells per region.
- Split the film into two layers. Draw the linework at 600ppi with a real minimum line of 0.6 to 0.7mm, which suits discharge on 110 mesh. Apply the 20ppi grid only to the grain and tone fills. Merge the two at 600ppi. The brief's 'everything on a 20ppi grid' instruction cannot coexist with its own line weights.
- Make the flame the light source and drop the lamp. In the logo the seed sits inside the flame, so draw the flame at logo proportion behind and around the seed's head. The flame then lights the desk and back-lights the seed, giving the face its figure-ground for free. Keep the laptop glow as a second, small light. This removes the lo-fi lamp trope, frees the crowded left strip, and makes the scene readable as the logo.
- Fix the scale problem explicitly. Make the seed about 8 to 9cm tall with a flame about 14cm tall that keeps its under-wrap, cut the window to a narrow 3cm slice at the far right edge, and keep only the laptop, one mug and the DAY 7 caption. Drop the sticky notes and the skyline micro-detail (cells of 1 to 2 are noise at 2m).
- Move the tally so it sits level with the wordmark baseline, to the right of the 'd' (shrink the wordmark to about 18cm if needed), or put it inside the panel at the lower right. Do not stack it 0.8cm under the last letters.
- Before paying for the full illustration, generate a 1:1, 26cm paper proof of a thumbnail of B from the corrected logo geometry. Pin it to a shirt and test it at 2 to 3m with 3 people who do not know the brand, side by side with a 1:1 proof of a corrected A. Pick the direction from the proofs, not from oversized AI renders.
- Show the founder both directions, as the spec requires: codex-B-back, codex-B-print-detail, codex-A-back and codex-A-print-detail, each labelled 'concept, to be redrawn'. Remove codex-worn from the set, or label it clearly as Back A with banned motifs (tuk-tuk, temple). Keep codex-pocket-detail and agy-front.
- Reframe the story away from a lone all-nighter, for the parent audience. Options: a second seed or a shared table edge to signal a team, or a dawn window instead of midnight, so Day 7 reads as shipping something, not burnout.
- Correct the agy-B note: its problem is a flood-filled panel at 65 to 75% coverage with the art knocked out to fabric, not a second ink.
