# PS[01]: first 10 (pilot run)

A run of 10 Dusk shirts for the team and core mentors. It doubles as the pre-production test for the run of 40: it proves the pattern, the print, the embroidery and the NFC tag before we spend on screens and minimum orders.

All prices are guesses. Replace them with quotes.

## Why 10 is made differently from 40

At 10 pieces, screen printing doesn't pay: every colour needs its own screen, so 5 colours × 2 sides is mostly setup cost. Methods with no setup cost win instead:

| Part | Run of 40 | Pilot of 10 |
|---|---|---|
| Sky print (back + hem) | Screen print, 4 colours + glow | **DTF transfer** (full colour, no screens, any pixel art) |
| Glow stars | Glow screen ink | Glow test on 2 shirts only (glow heat-transfer vinyl or one glow screen) |
| Neck number `07/40` | Screen + numbering | DTF: each of the 10 labels is unique at no extra cost |
| Pocket mascot | Embroidery | Embroidery (one-off digitising fee) |
| Role patch | PVC, MOQ ~50 | Embroidered TEAM patch, 10–20 pcs (or order 50 PVC now and keep the spares) |
| NFC label | Woven label, MOQ 100+ | Printed satin or twill-tape label, NFC tag folded inside |

## Route: cut-and-sew (recommended)

A small sample room cuts and sews from fabric we buy. The sky is pressed on the cut panels **before** sewing, so the horizon lines up across the side seams. It costs a bit more than blanks, but it's the only route that tests the real pattern for the 40.

**Fallback:** heavyweight black blanks from Bobae + a tailor adds the pocket, velcro and loop + DTF pressed on the finished shirt. It's faster, but the horizon breaks at the side seams and the fit is generic.

## Flow (about 4 weeks)

| When | Step | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Week 0 | Pick the 10 people and collect sizes (they try on a reference tee; don't ask them to guess). Generate print-ready files: back and front sky at real size, 10 numbered neck labels, the embroidery grid with thread colours. | Sizes and files locked |
| Week 1 | Buy fabric (240 gsm cotton jersey, nearest to aubergine-black, or black) and trims: loop velcro, orange cotton tape, thread. Order 20 NTAG215 laundry tags. **Strike-offs:** one DTF strip on our fabric, one embroidered pocket. | Swatch survives 5 washes; embroidery edges stay sharp |
| Week 2 | Sample room makes the pattern + **1 fit sample** (size M, no print). Try it on 3 people (S, M, XL builds). | Pattern signed off |
| Week 3 | Cut 10 sets. Press DTF on the back and front panels (mark the hem line on a jig so every panel lines up). Embroider 10 pockets. Sew, then attach the velcro, locker loop and TAP label. | 10 shirts sewn |
| Week 4 | QC every shirt. Write and **lock** the 10 tags, tap-test each. Wash one shirt 10 times as the torture test. Hand them out and collect a 5-question feedback form 2 weeks later. | 10 worn shirts, feedback in |

## What we learn before the run of 40

- **Print:** does the DTF sky crack or feel stiff after washing? Does the dither read at arm's length? If the sky looks good and holds up, screen print it for the 40.
- **Fit:** dropped shoulder and length on real bodies.
- **Pocket:** does anyone actually use it? Does the embroidered mascot sag the pocket?
- **NFC:** does the tag survive washing, and do people tap it? The `/t/[code]` route counts taps.
- **Glow:** worth a 5th screen, or drop it?

## Real-size print specs

- Half chest at size M is about 58 cm. The mockup's 6px sky cell becomes a **12 mm pixel** on the shirt: bold and readable from across a room.
- Stars: 6 mm squares, with the 5-pixel stars 18 mm across.
- Back sky band: full panel width × about 30 cm tall from the hem. Front band: the same height, matched at the side seams.
- Pocket mascot: 16 px × 3 mm = 48 mm wide; rows 0–11 show above the pocket edge, the rest is hidden inside.
- Files: transparent PNG at 300 dpi, scaled with nearest-neighbour only (no smoothing), plus SVG.

## Where to buy (Bangkok)

- **Fabric, blanks:** Bobae (โบ๊เบ๊), Pahurat (พาหุรัด)
- **Trims (velcro, tape, thread):** Sampeng (สำเพ็ง), Pahurat
- **DTF, embroidery, sample rooms:** around Bobae and Pratunam (ประตูน้ำ); many DTF shops take files over LINE
- **NFC laundry tags:** Shopee or Lazada (search NTAG215 laundry tag); test 2 before buying 20
