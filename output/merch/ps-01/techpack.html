02 · FRONT

  1. Seed in the pocket. The pixel logo, embroidered and peeking over the pocket edge. The pocket is real: 11 × 13 cm with a pen slot.
  2. Wrap-around horizon. Printed on the cut panels before sewing, so the sky lines up across the side seams.
  3. Patch panel on the left sleeve, 5 × 5 cm loop velcro. Your role patch comes with the shirt; round patches are earned.
  4. TAP label in the left side seam, with a washable NFC tag sealed inside. Opens what you're building now.
  5. Glow stars. The stars are printed in glow-in-the-dark ink, so after dark the shirt turns into its own night sky.
  6. Ember locker loop at the back neck. Hang it on a hook.

03 · DETAILS

A · SEED IN THE POCKET
The 16px logo with its disc removed, so only the flame and eyes show above the pocket. Embroidered in flat fill stitching, 3 mm per pixel, so the edges stay sharp.
B · PATCHES PVC, 5 × 5 cm, hook back
Pixel sprites like game achievements. Role patches come with the shirt. SHIFT[n] patches only go to people who mentored or shipped in that round, and are never sold.
C · HOW THE SKY PRINTS
No gradients. The sky is dithered in pixel steps, so each edition is 4 flat screen-print colours + 1 glow ink: cheaper, sharper, and true to the pixel style.
D · INSIDE NECK + TAP LABEL
The neck print uses SHIFT's bracket naming. The Thai line is a draft; run the multi-model review first.
