## Post

Some of the SHIFT feedback that stayed with me was about talking to people and changing your mind.

One student wrote:
“วันแรกไม่ค่อยกล้าคุยกับคนแปลกหน้า แต่ตอนนี้กล้าคุยกับคนแปลกหน้ามากขึ้น”

Another:
“การยอมรับว่าทุกไอเดียของเราไม่ได้ดีเสมอไป”

Talking to strangers. Accepting that your idea might need to change.

These are things I’m still learning as a founder.

Reading students describe those experiences makes me think about what we should measure alongside the finished project: who they spoke to, what surprised them, and what they changed afterward.

That’s something I want us to keep making room for at PassionSeed.
