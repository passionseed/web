# Product Doctrine

## 1. Core Philosophy: Self-Determination

At the core of PassionSeed is **Self-Determination**: the conviction that young people must author their own futures, rather than being passively steered by parental anxiety, tutor hype, or school pressure.

Traditional Thai education is cram-and-forget, pay-to-win, and overrun by passive certificate camps ("ค่ายนั่งฟังสะสมพอร์ต"). PassionSeed directly opposes this by anchoring on the three core pillars of Self-Determination Theory:

* **Autonomy**: The student chooses the problem, defines the hypothesis, and makes the call to pivot. Mentors guide; they never spoon-feed, dictate, or ghostwrite.
* **Competence**: Built through collision with reality, not arbitrary grades. An ugly working prototype tested by real humans beats a polished slide deck every single time. Failure data and pivot logs are celebrated as authentic learning assets.
* **Relatedness**: Surrounded by peers and alumni who treat self-directed building as normal, not exceptional.

---

## 2. The Strategy: The Trojan Horse Value Proposition

We operate with a deliberate **Trojan Horse** strategy:

* **The External Horse (The Commercial Wedge)**:
  * **What parents buy**: Immediate relief from admissions panic, standing out in TCAS Round 1, and a verifiable, uncheatable portfolio asset that beats thousands of generic certificate holders.
  * **What students buy**: A fast, exciting 7-day sprint to ship a live project with a cool prototype and a 1-page case study without sitting through boring lecture camps.
  * *Core Copy Rule*: Never sell abstract philosophy to the market. Parents and students do not pay for "Self-Determination Theory". They pay for an urgent, high-stakes admission outcome.
* **The Internal Payload (The Authentic Transformation)**:
  * **What is smuggled inside**: **Self-determination, agency, and trajectory shift**.
  * Once inside the sandbox, students cannot passively spectate or outsource the work. They are forced into direct collision with reality: locking an atomic scope, shipping an ugly MVP to real external users, absorbing failure data, and defending their pivots.
  * They enroll to get a TCAS portfolio project; they graduate as self-directed builders who own their decisions.

## 3. The Product Ladder: TechSeed → SHIFT

*(Note: ProjectSeed is paused for now. All commercial and curriculum focus is on this two-stage sequence.)*

### Stage 1: TechSeed (The Spark & Builder Foundation)
* **Goal**: Shift students from passive consumers to active creators.
* **Format**: Hands-on builder immersion where students learn that building functional hardware and software is within their reach.
* **Community Flywheel**: TechSeed alumni return voluntarily to mentor subsequent cohorts (e.g., Kyzz), providing our scalable capacity and social proof.

### Stage 2: SHIFT (The 7-Day Proof-of-Work Sandbox)
* **Surface**: `/shift`
* **Price**: **2x price** (~฿1,980 to ฿2,000, up from the preliminary ฿990 tier) to ensure skin-in-the-game, filter for committed builders, and reflect intense mentor review.
* **Core Deliverable**:
  * **Day 1**: Scope Lock & Atomic Hypothesis (cut 80% of excess features, lock 1 page).
  * **Day 3-5**: The Ugly Ship & Pivot Check (deploy zero-code or hardware MVP to external users; log failure data).
  * **Day 7**: Reality Collision & 1-Page Case Study (includes an uncheatable Pivot Log for university admissions/TCAS portfolios).
* **Tagline**: *"เลิกสะสมใบเซอร์ค่ายนั่งฟัง แล้วมาสร้าง Live Project พร้อม Pivot Log และ 1-Page TCAS Case Study"*

## 4. Users & Audiences

* **The Student (Primary User / Dawn theme)**:
  * Motivated by discovery, autonomy, and making things that actually work.
  * Tone: Conversational, warm, peer-to-peer. Never lecture, never talk down, no corporate jargon.
* **The Parent (Economic Buyer)**:
  * Motivated by peace of mind, fear of wasted time/tuition, and seeking genuine capability for their child.
  * Tone: Credible, clear, transparent. Shows evidence of real problem-solving and character growth rather than empty admission guarantees.
* **The Alumni Mentor (Dusk theme)**:
  * Past students who lived the transition and return to give back.
  * Tone: Reflective, seasoned, respectful of the mentor-builder dynamic.

---

## 5. Design & Brand Principles

1. **The student's work is the star**: Connect outside visitors and admissions readers to authentic, self-authored work.
2. **Reality collision over polished fiction**: Highlight what broke, what was learned, and why pivots occurred. A raw pivot log is 10x more credible than a generic certificate.
3. **Atmospheric themes (Dawn & Dusk)**:
   * **Dawn**: Student-facing. Cool blues warming into gold. Exploratory and optimistic.
   * **Dusk**: Expert and mentor-facing. Deep violets cooling into amber. Warm and authoritative.
4. **Copy rules**:
   * No em dashes (—) in user-facing copy. Use a comma, a colon, or rewrite the sentence.
   * Thai-first for student interaction; bilingual where helpful.
