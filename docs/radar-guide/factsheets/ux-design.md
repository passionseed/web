# UX/UI Designer (นักออกแบบ UX/UI): fact sheet for Level 1

- Guide: `lib/radar-guides/ux-design/` (draft, as of 2026-11-20). Level 1 is dream, work, tonight, next; round 1 opens Level 2, then the DB deck.
- Built from the DB dump of the `ux-design` field (researched 2026-06-19: 18 cards, 15 sources, research JSON). That research is mostly salary and market data, which stays on the Level 2 deck. Level 1 needed new sources: every one below was opened on 2026-11-19.
- Grades: **A** primary source we checked, exact wording. **B** secondary source with a date (news report, feature interview). **C** one person's account, a blog or a seller, or no source. **X** do not say. Level 1 prints A and B; one C claim is printed only with the pending badge and the words "บริษัทยังไม่ได้ยืนยัน".

## Safety and tone notes

- Tonight: the student screenshots only their own app and covers names, numbers, addresses and chats; the person testing is someone at home or a friend, and nobody is photographed or recorded (fixed `safety` block and a fixed line in the write-up). The write-up stays on the device.
- No 1:1 adult and student contact anywhere: the tester is family or a friend, chosen by the student.
- The LINE MAN intern claim names no person and carries the pending badge.
- Copy was drafted, then rewritten with Gemini 4 Neon (Antigravity CLI); every line was checked against the house rules (no dashes, no น้องๆ, box lengths) and the facts above. The Cursor and Codex Thai review from AGENTS.md is still to do before the guide goes live.
