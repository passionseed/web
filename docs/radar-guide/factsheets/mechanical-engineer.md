## Kinds of mechanical engineer (work level)

The five tiles (แอร์และพัดลม, รถยนต์และ EV, โรงงานผลิต, หุ่นยนต์, พลังงาน) are qualitative and carry no numbers. Cars and cooling are the two biggest Thai examples (facts 3 and 4); automotive, robotics and mechatronics are real programme fields on mytcas (fact 7). แอร์และพัดลม is the kind the work game just played (the stand fan).

## Safety and tone notes

- Tonight is a teardown: only things with no plug, no battery and nothing sharp (the ideas card: a click pen, a clothes peg, a soap-pump head, a toy car with no battery). Stop if it has to be forced or pried open. Take it apart piece by piece on a tray and watch for springs. Never open a fan or any electrical appliance: it may only be looked at from outside, unplugged. Ask the owner first if it is not the student's own. The pinned safety block says all of this; the write-up's fixed line says no plug, no battery, nothing sharp.
- The write-up stays on the device. Excalidraw is optional.
- Students are minors: no 1:1 contact anywhere; the ending is follow and save, with SHIFT as one line (shared outro).
- Never "น้องๆ", no dashes, no prices, no admission promises (shared round 1 level).
- Cross-review done on 2026-11-20: Codex for logic, Cursor for Thai, and every fact re-checked at its source. It changed: ทีมวิจัยจาก สจล. instead of KMITL-led, the 22% body weight taken out of copy, ก่อนส่งมอบ instead of ก่อนให้คนขึ้น, the eco-car's distance as an equivalent, the air-conditioner rank as Thailand's, a stand fan instead of a desk fan, and safer teardown ideas (a soap-pump head and a toy car with no battery instead of a stapler and a wind-up toy).
