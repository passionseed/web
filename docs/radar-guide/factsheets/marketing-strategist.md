## Safety and tone notes

- Butterbear is told from the news. The dream's honesty card says "เรื่องนี้เล่าจากข่าว เราไม่ได้ทำงานกับแบรนด์นี้", and `banned.ts` blocks wording that implies a partnership or sponsorship. No logo or mascot likeness in the art.
- Tonight is free and needs no account. Students are minors: the pinned safety line says not to message the shop or talk to strangers; the plan uses only what they see themselves and what friends say. Tonight is asking friends (no names) and writing the plan; counting happens the next day after school, with a friend, numbers only and no photos of anyone. Taking the plan to the shop means going with a teacher or parent (the pinned line and the ending both say so). The write-up stays on the device.
- No price or baht figure anywhere on Level 1. The work level's "ลดราคา" answer is a concept, not a number.

It changed: engagement instead of "คนพูดถึง", timing and opportunity instead of luck for the Chula lecturer, ส่วนใหญ่ instead of "รักที่สุด", the queue told in the past, a "wrong place or time" bin, the cat clip's comments asking where the shop is, and tonight's order (counting moves to the next day, with a friend).
