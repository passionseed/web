# Accountant (นักบัญชี): fact sheet for the Level 1 guide

- Guide: `lib/radar-guides/accountant/` (draft, as_of 2026-11-20): dream, work, tonight, next (Level 1), round1 (Level 2), then the DB deck.
- Built from the DB dump of 2026-11-20 (field row, 12 cards, 8 sources, research JSON, tier `exposed`). Every claim the guide uses was re-opened at its source on 2026-11-20, and re-checked in the cross-review the same day (see the last section).
- Angle: AI now does the typing; the accountant is the one who finds what doesn't add up and tells a business the truth about its money. Honest about the WEF outlook, never scary, never a promise.
- Grades: **A** primary source we opened, exact wording. **B** secondary source with a date, or a company describing its own kind of product. **C** our estimate, a blog, or no source. **X** do not use. Only A and B appear in the guide.

## Safety and tone notes

- Students are minors: tonight uses their own money or a club's money with the club teacher's OK; real amounts, names and account numbers are never posted anywhere (a portfolio needs only the method and what they found); the write-up never leaves the device; and the only sign-up mentioned is "if you already have a Google account".
- No 1:1 contact anywhere; the ending is follow/save, share, other careers and the SHIFT line from the shared outro.
- The STARK story is told as what the special audit found and what the SEC alleged; the honesty card says this is only the start of the case and that guilt waits for the investigation and the courts.
- Thai lines were drafted, rewritten by Gemini 4 Neon (Antigravity CLI) and checked against the house rules (no dashes, no น้องๆ, เธอ, box lengths).
- Cross-review done on 2026-11-20: Codex for logic, Cursor for Thai, and every fact re-checked at its source. It changed: the STARK story kept to what the audit found and what the SEC alleged, "some" accounting software instead of all, the WEF line scoped to the employers who answered and to 2030, the third bin named รายจ่ายส่วนตัว, "หลักสูตรสายบัญชี" for the 94, and names and account numbers added to tonight's privacy line.
