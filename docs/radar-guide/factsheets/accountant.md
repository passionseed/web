# Accountant (นักบัญชี): fact sheet for the Level 1 guide

- Guide: `lib/radar-guides/accountant/` (draft, as_of 2026-11-20): dream, work, tonight, next (Level 1), round1 (Level 2), then the DB deck.
- Built from the DB dump of 2026-11-20 (field row, 12 cards, 8 sources, research JSON, tier `exposed`). Every claim the guide uses was re-opened at its source on 2026-11-20.
- Angle: AI now does the typing; the accountant is the one who finds what doesn't add up and tells a business the truth about its money. Honest about the WEF outlook, never scary, never a promise.
- Grades: **A** primary source we opened, exact wording. **B** secondary source with a date, or a company describing its own kind of product. **C** our estimate, a blog, or no source. **X** do not use. Only A and B appear in the guide.

## Safety and tone notes

- Students are minors: tonight uses their own money or a club's money with the club teacher's OK, real amounts stay private, the write-up never leaves the device, and the only sign-up mentioned is "if you already have a Google account".
- No 1:1 contact anywhere; the ending is follow/save, share, other careers and the SHIFT line from the shared outro.
- The STARK story is told as what the special audit found and what the SEC alleged, with "คดียังไม่จบ".
- Thai lines were drafted, rewritten by Gemini 4 Neon (Antigravity CLI) and checked against the house rules (no dashes, no น้องๆ, เธอ, box lengths). The Cursor and Codex Thai review from AGENTS.md is still to do before this guide goes live.
