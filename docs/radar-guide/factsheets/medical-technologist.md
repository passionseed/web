## Tonight's first step

Turn a phone into a magnifier with one drop of water, run a blind test (a family member labels salt and sugar A and B), look and photograph without tasting, and write a short lab note: sample, preparation, what you saw, what could fool you (bubbles, dust), and how to make it more accurate. Free, at home, under an hour, kitchen items only. The note stays on the device (EvidenceMaker). Next step offered: borrow the school lab's microscope.
