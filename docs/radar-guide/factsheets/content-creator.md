- Level 1: dream (Moo Deng and her keeper), work (a 15-second clip for the student's regular roti shop, deliberately not one at school so the clip does not show where they study), tonight (plan and shoot a 15-second clip with a phone), next. Round 1 opens Level 2, then the DB deck.

## Safety and tone

- Students are minors. The work game teaches: cut a stranger's face from a clip; never answer a stranger asking for your school or phone number (replying is wrong, not a near miss): block them and tell an adult you trust; check a friend's shirt shows no school name. Tonight: film objects, hands or a corner at home; ask anyone before filming them; no school name, name tag, house number or location; no need to post; talk to a parent before posting one day.
- No promise of fame or money (banned patterns). The dream says Moo Deng was not famous only because of luck ("ไม่ได้ดังแค่เพราะดวง"), the host says nobody knows which clip will take off but storytelling can be practised, and the honest card says fame rises and falls. The ending's "may not suit you if" names redoing the same clip again and again, and not wanting to check the numbers and read comments every day, even on days a clip flops.
- No tool sign-up: tonight uses the phone's camera and its photo or gallery app only.
- Cross-review done on 2026-11-20: Codex for logic, Cursor for Thai, and every fact re-checked at its source. It changed: replying to a stranger who asks for your school and phone is now wrong, not a near miss; nothing in the missions shows a school or home (the student's regular roti shop, filming a corner at home, a friend's shirt checked for a school name); Moo Deng's visitor numbers are credited to the zoo director; the drop at second 9 is the shop-sign shot held too long, not a menu shot; fame is not promised.
